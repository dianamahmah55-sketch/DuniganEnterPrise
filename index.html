Frequently Asked Questions

Is remote support safe?

Yes. We use reputable, encrypted screen-sharing tools, and every session starts only when you approve it. You can see what we're doing and end the session whenever you want. We won't ask for your passwords unless absolutely necessary.

What does remote management install on my computer?

A small management tool that lets us run updates and security checks and, with your approval, connect for repairs. We install it only with your permission, we tell you what it does, and we remove it if you cancel.

What if you can't fix it remotely?

Some problems, like a failed hard drive or broken screen, need hands-on repair. We'll tell you up front and can schedule an on-site visit or advise you on the best option.

Should I back up my files first?

Yes, whenever possible. We recommend backing up important files before any repair, and we'll remind you before we make risky changes.

What areas do you serve on-site?

We're based in Cleveland, MS. Call or text 662-402-9032 to ask whether we can come to your location. Remote support is available anywhere.

Do you work on Macs?

Our focus is Windows computers. Contact us about other devices and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

How do I cancel a monthly plan?

You can cancel any time before your next billing date by emailing or texting us. See the Terms below for details.

How do I pay?

Plans and remote sessions are paid securely online through Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
