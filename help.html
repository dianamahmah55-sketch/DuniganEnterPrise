Fix-It Guides

Fix common computer problems yourself.

Plain-language steps for the problems we see most. Try these first, and if they don't solve it, call or text us at 662-402-9032.

In this guide

  • 2. My Wi-Fi keeps dropping
  • 3. My printer says it's offline
  • 4. My computer is running slow
  • 5. My email isn't syncing or sending

1. I forgot my password or I'm locked out

  1. On the sign-in page, click Forgot password (or Can't access your account).
  2. Enter your work email and follow the code or link sent to your email or phone.
  3. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters. A few random words works well, for example: blue-kettle-orbit-seven.
  4. Sign in again on every device that asks, including your phone's mail app.
  5. If you never receive the code, check your spam folder and wait five minutes before trying again.
Call or text us at 662-402-9032 if you no longer have access to the recovery email or phone, if the account says it is disabled, or if you did not request the reset. An unrequested reset can mean someone else is trying to get in.

2. My Wi-Fi keeps dropping

  1. Check whether other devices drop too. If only one does, skip to step 5.
  2. Unplug the router and modem, wait 30 seconds, then plug the modem in first and the router a minute later. Wait three minutes.
  3. Move closer to the router for a test. If the connection holds, the signal is too weak where you were.
  4. Check that the router is off the floor, away from microwaves and not tucked inside a cabinet.
  5. On the affected device, open Wi-Fi settings, choose Forget this network, then reconnect with the Wi-Fi password.
  6. Turn the device off and on again.
Call or text us at 662-402-9032 if it drops several times a day after these steps, if the whole office is affected, or if the router is more than five years old.

3. My printer says it's offline

  1. Make sure the printer is on, shows no error light and has paper.
  2. Check that the printer is on the same Wi-Fi network as your computer, or that its USB cable is firmly plugged in.
  3. Restart the printer, then restart your computer.
  4. Windows: open Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners, select the printer and click Open print queue. Cancel any stuck jobs, then in the Printer menu make sure Use Printer Offline is not ticked.
  5. Mac: open System Settings > Printers & Scanners, remove the printer with the minus button, then add it again with the plus button.
  6. Print a test page.
Call or text us at 662-402-9032 if the printer still shows offline after being removed and re-added, or if several people can't print.

4. My computer is running slow

  1. Save your work and restart. A computer left on for weeks slows down, and this fixes it more often than anything else.
  2. Close browser tabs you aren't using. Twenty or more open tabs can use most of the memory.
  3. Check free storage space. If less than 10% of the drive is free, delete large files you don't need or move them to cloud storage.
  4. Windows: press Ctrl + Shift + Esc, open Task Manager and look at what uses the most CPU or memory. Don't end anything you don't recognise.
  5. Windows: in Settings > Windows Update, install any waiting updates and restart. Mac: do the same under System Settings > General > Software Update.
  6. Windows: in Task Manager's Startup apps tab, switch off programs you don't need at sign-in.
Call or text us at 662-402-9032 if it stays slow after a restart and an update, if it makes grinding or clicking noises, or if it is more than five years old with a hard disk rather than a solid-state drive.

5. My email isn't syncing or sending

  1. Check your internet connection by opening any website.
  2. Look in your Outbox folder. A message stuck there is usually a large attachment; remove it or share a link instead.
  3. Close and reopen the mail app, then restart your computer or phone.
  4. Try the same account in a web browser. If the browser works, the problem is the app, not the account.
  5. If the account asks for a password, enter it again. A recent password change is the most common cause.
  6. On a phone, remove the account and add it back.
Call or text us at 662-402-9032 if the browser version doesn't work either, if you see messages about the account being blocked or over its storage limit, or if mail you send is bouncing back.
